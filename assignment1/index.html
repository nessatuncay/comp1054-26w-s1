Flares of the Blazing Sun

About The Song

The song is from the game Honkai: Star Rail. It tells and shows the anger, frustration and resemtment a character, Phainon, after he was forced to live through 33,550,336 cycles where he had to take the lives of those he cared about just so there could be a chance to save them in the end. When he learned that his world, Amphoreus, was a simulation to create a being, Irontomb, powerful enough to destroy the universe by simulating this world and turning everyone into a power source. Phainon had to make the difficult choice of reversing time at the end of each cycle just so the simulation couldn't be completed and everyone wouldn't turn into a power source. But the only way to reverse time and restart the cycle was by killing the people he loved and cared about, and saw as a family. This song tells about his anger when at the end of the 33,550,336th cycle Phainon was able to gain enough power to break free from the simulation just for a short moment. And in that short moment he directed his anger and hatred towards the god of Destruction, a deity whose goal is to destroy the universe, as Irontomb was being created to serve THEIR will. Phainon was able to strike at the god of Destruction and made a small cut on THEIR face before his power and adrenaline wore off and he fell back into the simulation where the cycle began once more for the last time.
